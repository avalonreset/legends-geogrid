# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in `legends-geogrid` and the `cto-legends` ecosystem a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

- Focus on technical accuracy, reproducible evidence, and clear documentation.
- Respect contributor time, user data boundaries, and upstream open-source attributions.
- Avoid personal attacks, trolling, or disruptive behavior in issues and pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns through the channels listed in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) or [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
