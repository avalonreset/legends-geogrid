# Parked Citation Research

## Product Takeaway

GeoGrid rank heatmaps and local citations are adjacent local SEO workflows, not the same data product.

- GeoGrid heatmaps answer: "Where does this business rank for this keyword from each nearby coordinate?"
- Citation audits answer: "Is the business name, address, phone, website, category, and profile data consistent across directories and data aggregators?"
- Citation fulfillment answers: "Can we submit, correct, monitor, or renew those listings across publishers?"

The current prototype should not mix citation fulfillment into the core GeoGrid dashboard. If we revisit citations later, the safest sequence is:

1. Heatmap rank proof through DataForSEO Google Maps SERP.
2. Citation/NAP opportunity audit.
3. Optional fulfillment routing to a vendor, aggregator account, or manual ops queue.

## What The Market Is Selling

LeadSnap positions citations as a listing-distribution and monitoring product. Its citations page says it handles local citations across trusted directories, imports data from Google Business Profile, monitors across 42 directories, and starts at $20/month per location.

Search Atlas positions its local citation builder around aggregator distribution. Its public page highlights Data Axle, Foursquare, Neustar Localeze, Yellow Pages Network, and GPS providers, with one-time payment, 12-month verification, and up to 20% discount when selecting all five networks. A Search Atlas article also says citation submissions are available at $25 each for 12 months with additional-directory discounts.

BrightLocal positions citation building as a one-time-pay service where the business owns the citations, gets login access, and can receive white-label reporting.

## Possible Future Add-On Shape

### Phase 1: Audit Layer

No special aggregator relationship required.

- Extract canonical NAP from website, LocalBusiness schema, and GBP data if available.
- Check tier-one citation presence manually or through search/API workflows.
- Score citation gaps by business category and market.
- Show a watchlist in the lead report: Google Business Profile, Apple Business / Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, BBB, Facebook, Data Axle, Foursquare, Neustar Localeze, Yellow Pages Network.
- Attach recommendation language: "audit in GeoGrid, fulfill through chosen provider or internal ops."

### Phase 2: Evidence Layer

Useful for sales demos and retainers.

- Store screenshots or API responses for found/missing profiles.
- Show duplicate risk and NAP mismatch flags.
- Add a citation readiness block to the same lead report as the heatmap.

### Phase 3: Fulfillment Layer

Only build after deciding the commercial path.

- Vendor-assisted: generate order sheets for LeadSnap, BrightLocal, Search Atlas, or another service.
- Manual ops: create a tracked checklist with credentials, submission URLs, and proof receipts.
- Direct aggregator: only if we have accounts/API/partner access and understand renewal rules.

## Cost Framing

The $20-$40 citation conversation is realistic as a fulfillment anchor, but it should not be compared directly to DataForSEO heatmap rank-data cost.

- DataForSEO heatmap cost is per SERP coordinate.
- Citation fulfillment cost is labor, directory access, aggregator distribution, monitoring, cleanup, and reporting.
- GeoGrid can reduce waste by showing when citation work is actually necessary before paying a vendor.
