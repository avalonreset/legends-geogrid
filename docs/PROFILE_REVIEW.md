# Business profile, customer need, measured visibility

The researched workflow always creates a public profile review from its validated
identity source. `study validate` returns it; `study assemble` carries it to the
report. It includes observed name, category, address, phone, website, additional
categories, rating summary, hours and customer links. Missing fields are unknown,
not missing-profile defects. There is no invented completeness score.

Each observed field retains source ID, exact JSON pointer, source hash and
observation date. The report renderer checks structure, not source authenticity;
the study validator checks lineage. The agent still checks interpretation.
Legacy standalone diagnostic reports may lack this review; do not present them
as completed researched business studies.

## Required agent review

1. Establish the exact listing and business type: physical premises, service-area
   business or hybrid. Do not infer a public storefront from a provider coordinate.
2. Reconcile profile, official website and relevant booking/menu/ordering links.
   Record contradictory information and competing explanations. Ask the owner
   only when the answer cannot responsibly be established from public evidence.
3. Connect each selected search to a verified offering, a customer need, local
   result intent and its distinct value. Retain offering citations and rejected
   alternatives. Report why these queries matter, not just their volume.
4. Inspect customer links relevant to that need. A returned URL is not proof that
   it works, that its address is current, or that a transaction succeeds. Preserve
   a dated fetched result before calling a link broken or inconsistent.
5. Use competitors repeatedly present at weak origins to form focused questions.
   Record exact queries, coordinates, denominators and how many competitor results
   were retained. Inspect relevant public evidence before proposing an explanation.
   Avoid generic reviews/categories/content prescriptions or causal claims.

Private GBP performance, calls, enquiries and orders are optional owner-provided
evidence. Without them, commercial effects remain unknown. Public star rating and
count do not establish review recency, authenticity, sentiment or ranking impact.

## Decision classes

- `business_information`: confirm or correct established customer-facing facts.
- `measurement`: repair acquisition, densify, extend or remeasure with a stated
  question and incremental cost. A proposed expansion is not collected data.
- `investigation`: investigate a supported question before prescribing an edit.
- `maintain`: preserve a useful benchmark and monitor instead of changing things.

Every action still requires evidence, owner, uncertainty and a success check.
Prioritize identity and customer harm before speculative ranking work. Keep all
actions proposed until the owner or an authorized workflow records execution.
Bank profile context and actions with the study; link later monitoring to its
parent, preserve changed settings and separate rank outcomes from conversions.

This is targeted research for a local visibility decision, not a full website
audit. It makes no universal ranking-factor weights or revenue predictions.
